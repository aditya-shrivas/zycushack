# FAQ: Why is the currency conversion slower for large business units?

## Answer
This depends on the role configuration. Review the role's permissions in Admin > Roles.

## Details
Collect the tenant ID, the affected record ID, the exact error text and the time of failure in UTC before escalating. Most cases are resolved by a tenant administrator without engineering involvement. This article applies to tenants on release 26.1 and later. Earlier releases display the same behaviour but use a different admin menu path.

Most cases are resolved by a tenant administrator without engineering involvement. The issue affects only the tenant where the configuration was changed. Other tenants on the same release are not affected. Customers typically report this when a currency conversion is processed in bulk or immediately after an administrator changes Spend Analytics settings.
