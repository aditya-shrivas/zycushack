# FAQ: Can we restrict who edits a dashboard?

## Answer
Yes. An administrator can enable this under the module settings; the change applies to new records.

## Details
Customers typically report this when a dashboard is processed in bulk or immediately after an administrator changes Spend Analytics settings. Collect the tenant ID, the affected record ID, the exact error text and the time of failure in UTC before escalating. If more than one tenant reports the same error within 24 hours, treat it as a potential platform incident and check the Known Issues list before troubleshooting individually.

This article applies to tenants on release 26.1 and later. Earlier releases display the same behaviour but use a different admin menu path.
