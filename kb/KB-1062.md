# FAQ: Why does the SAML assertion show a different status for some users?

## Answer
Retention follows the tenant's data retention policy, which is 7 years by default.

## Details
Customers typically report this when a SAML assertion is processed in bulk or immediately after an administrator changes Platform settings. Most cases are resolved by a tenant administrator without engineering involvement. If more than one tenant reports the same error within 24 hours, treat it as a potential platform incident and check the Known Issues list before troubleshooting individually.
