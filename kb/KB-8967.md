# FAQ: Why does the role permission show a different status for some users?

## Answer
Yes. An administrator can enable this under the module settings; the change applies to new records.

## Details
This article applies to tenants on release 26.1 and later. Earlier releases display the same behaviour but use a different admin menu path. Customers typically report this when a role permission is processed in bulk or immediately after an administrator changes Platform settings. Collect the tenant ID, the affected record ID, the exact error text and the time of failure in UTC before escalating.
