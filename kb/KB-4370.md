# How to: Configure weighted scoring

## Before you start
Collect the tenant ID, the affected record ID, the exact error text and the time of failure in UTC before escalating. Customers typically report this when a record is processed in bulk or immediately after an administrator changes Sourcing settings. Most cases are resolved by a tenant administrator without engineering involvement.

## Steps
1. Sign in as a Sourcing administrator.
2. Go to Sourcing > Settings.
3. Select the option for: configure weighted scoring.
4. Enter the required values and save.
5. Verify the change on a test record before announcing it to users.
