# How to: Delegate approvals while out of office

## Steps
1. Open your profile menu and select Out of Office.
2. Choose a delegate with the same or higher approval limit.
3. Set the start and end dates. Delegation ends automatically on the end date.
4. Optional: tick "Notify delegate" to send an email to the delegate.

## Notes
Delegation applies to requisitions, purchase orders and invoices routed to you during the selected dates. Documents already pending before the start date stay with you unless you reassign them from Workflow > Pending.
