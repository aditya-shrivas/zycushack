# FAQ: Can we export supplier profile data on a schedule?

## Answer
Retention follows the tenant's data retention policy, which is 7 years by default.

## Details
Customers typically report this when a supplier profile is processed in bulk or immediately after an administrator changes Supplier Management settings. Most cases are resolved by a tenant administrator without engineering involvement. If more than one tenant reports the same error within 24 hours, treat it as a potential platform incident and check the Known Issues list before troubleshooting individually.

This article applies to tenants on release 26.1 and later. Earlier releases display the same behaviour but use a different admin menu path. If more than one tenant reports the same error within 24 hours, treat it as a potential platform incident and check the Known Issues list before troubleshooting individually. Collect the tenant ID, the affected record ID, the exact error text and the time of failure in UTC before escalating.
