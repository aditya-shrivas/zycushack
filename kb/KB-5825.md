# FAQ: Why is the certificate expiry alert slower for large business units?

## Answer
No. This is a platform limit. Log a feature request if the business need is significant.

## Details
Most cases are resolved by a tenant administrator without engineering involvement. Customers typically report this when a certificate expiry alert is processed in bulk or immediately after an administrator changes Supplier Management settings. Collect the tenant ID, the affected record ID, the exact error text and the time of failure in UTC before escalating.

This article applies to tenants on release 26.1 and later. Earlier releases display the same behaviour but use a different admin menu path. The issue affects only the tenant where the configuration was changed. Other tenants on the same release are not affected.
