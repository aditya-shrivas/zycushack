# FAQ: Who receives notifications for a webhook?

## Answer
Retention follows the tenant's data retention policy, which is 7 years by default.

## Details
Collect the tenant ID, the affected record ID, the exact error text and the time of failure in UTC before escalating. The issue affects only the tenant where the configuration was changed. Other tenants on the same release are not affected. Customers typically report this when a webhook is processed in bulk or immediately after an administrator changes Integrations settings.

Collect the tenant ID, the affected record ID, the exact error text and the time of failure in UTC before escalating. This article applies to tenants on release 26.1 and later. Earlier releases display the same behaviour but use a different admin menu path. Most cases are resolved by a tenant administrator without engineering involvement.
