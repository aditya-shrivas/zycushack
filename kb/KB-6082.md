# Approvals appear in the approver inbox with a delay

## Symptoms
Approvers report that requisitions or purchase orders reach their inbox minutes or hours after submission. No error is shown.

## Cause
The workflow queue processes approvals in batches. Delays above 15 minutes usually mean the queue is backed up by a large number of auto-approval rules, or the Workflow Dispatcher job interval was increased.

## Resolution
1. Open Admin > Scheduler > Workflow Dispatcher and confirm the interval is 5 minutes or less.
2. Review auto-approval rules; consolidate rules with overlapping conditions.
3. If delays exceed 2 hours, escalate to L2 with the queue depth screenshot.

## Notes
Sudden approval delays at a strategic account are often an early signal of wider adoption problems. Check the account's usage trend and notify the TAM when usage is also declining.
