# FAQ: Can we restrict who edits a clause library?

## Answer
Yes. An administrator can enable this under the module settings; the change applies to new records.

## Details
Most cases are resolved by a tenant administrator without engineering involvement. Customers typically report this when a clause library is processed in bulk or immediately after an administrator changes Contracts settings. Collect the tenant ID, the affected record ID, the exact error text and the time of failure in UTC before escalating.

Most cases are resolved by a tenant administrator without engineering involvement. This article applies to tenants on release 26.1 and later. Earlier releases display the same behaviour but use a different admin menu path. The issue affects only the tenant where the configuration was changed. Other tenants on the same release are not affected.
