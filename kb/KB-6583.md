# FAQ: Can we restrict who edits a RFx event?

## Answer
No. This is a platform limit. Log a feature request if the business need is significant.

## Details
If more than one tenant reports the same error within 24 hours, treat it as a potential platform incident and check the Known Issues list before troubleshooting individually. Collect the tenant ID, the affected record ID, the exact error text and the time of failure in UTC before escalating. The issue affects only the tenant where the configuration was changed. Other tenants on the same release are not affected.
