# FAQ: Who receives notifications for a supplier profile?

## Answer
Retention follows the tenant's data retention policy, which is 7 years by default.

## Details
If more than one tenant reports the same error within 24 hours, treat it as a potential platform incident and check the Known Issues list before troubleshooting individually. Customers typically report this when a supplier profile is processed in bulk or immediately after an administrator changes Supplier Management settings. Collect the tenant ID, the affected record ID, the exact error text and the time of failure in UTC before escalating.
