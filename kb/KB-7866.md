# FAQ: Who receives notifications for a credit memo?

## Answer
Retention follows the tenant's data retention policy, which is 7 years by default.

## Details
This article applies to tenants on release 26.1 and later. Earlier releases display the same behaviour but use a different admin menu path. Collect the tenant ID, the affected record ID, the exact error text and the time of failure in UTC before escalating. Customers typically report this when a credit memo is processed in bulk or immediately after an administrator changes eInvoice settings.
