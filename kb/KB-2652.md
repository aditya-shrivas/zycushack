# FAQ: Who receives notifications for a catalog item?

## Answer
This depends on the role configuration. Review the role's permissions in Admin > Roles.

## Details
Customers typically report this when a catalog item is processed in bulk or immediately after an administrator changes eProcurement settings. Most cases are resolved by a tenant administrator without engineering involvement. If more than one tenant reports the same error within 24 hours, treat it as a potential platform incident and check the Known Issues list before troubleshooting individually.
