# FAQ: Why is the contract template slower for large business units?

## Answer
No. This is a platform limit. Log a feature request if the business need is significant.

## Details
This article applies to tenants on release 26.1 and later. Earlier releases display the same behaviour but use a different admin menu path. Collect the tenant ID, the affected record ID, the exact error text and the time of failure in UTC before escalating. Most cases are resolved by a tenant administrator without engineering involvement.
