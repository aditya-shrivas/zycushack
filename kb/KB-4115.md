# E-1281: Purchase order does not trigger

## Symptoms
Users see error **E-1281** when a purchase order does not trigger. The record remains in its previous state and no notification is sent.

## Cause
This error is caused by a paused background scheduler job.

## Resolution
1. Open Admin > Scheduler and locate the paused job.
2. Resume the job and run it once manually.
3. Confirm downstream records update within 15 minutes.

## Escalation criteria
Escalate to L2 Engineering if the resolution steps fail, if more than 50 records are affected, or if the customer reports the issue on multiple tenants.

## Notes
Most cases are resolved by a tenant administrator without engineering involvement. The issue affects only the tenant where the configuration was changed. Other tenants on the same release are not affected. Collect the tenant ID, the affected record ID, the exact error text and the time of failure in UTC before escalating.
