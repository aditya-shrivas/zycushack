# E-5104: Supplier onboarding form is rejected

## Symptoms
Users see error **E-5104** when a supplier onboarding form is rejected. The record remains in its previous state and no notification is sent.

## Cause
This error is caused by an expired integration credential.

## Resolution
1. Open Admin > Integrations > Credentials.
2. Regenerate the credential and update it in the connected system.
3. Trigger a manual sync and confirm the job status shows Completed.

## Escalation criteria
Escalate to L2 Engineering if the resolution steps fail, if more than 50 records are affected, or if the customer reports the issue on multiple tenants.

## Notes
The issue affects only the tenant where the configuration was changed. Other tenants on the same release are not affected. Customers typically report this when a supplier onboarding form is processed in bulk or immediately after an administrator changes Supplier Management settings. Collect the tenant ID, the affected record ID, the exact error text and the time of failure in UTC before escalating.
