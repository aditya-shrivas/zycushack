# FAQ: Does the purchase order support multiple currencies?

## Answer
No. This is a platform limit. Log a feature request if the business need is significant.

## Details
Most cases are resolved by a tenant administrator without engineering involvement. Customers typically report this when a purchase order is processed in bulk or immediately after an administrator changes eProcurement settings. If more than one tenant reports the same error within 24 hours, treat it as a potential platform incident and check the Known Issues list before troubleshooting individually.

Most cases are resolved by a tenant administrator without engineering involvement. Collect the tenant ID, the affected record ID, the exact error text and the time of failure in UTC before escalating. The issue affects only the tenant where the configuration was changed. Other tenants on the same release are not affected.
