# FAQ: Why does the scoring sheet show a different status for some users?

## Answer
Retention follows the tenant's data retention policy, which is 7 years by default.

## Details
Collect the tenant ID, the affected record ID, the exact error text and the time of failure in UTC before escalating. Customers typically report this when a scoring sheet is processed in bulk or immediately after an administrator changes Sourcing settings. The issue affects only the tenant where the configuration was changed. Other tenants on the same release are not affected.
