# Bulk approval limits (by design)

## Limit
Bulk approval accepts a maximum of **200 documents per batch**. Selecting more than 200 disables the Approve button. This is a deliberate limit that protects approval audit trails and is not a defect.

## Workaround
1. Filter the approval list (for example by supplier or date) to batches of 200 or fewer.
2. Approve each batch.

## Product feedback
Requests to raise the limit should be logged as feature requests and routed to Product with the customer's typical batch size.
