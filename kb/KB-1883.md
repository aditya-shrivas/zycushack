# Configuration reference: Bank detail change notification

## Location
Admin > Supplier Management > Settings > Bank detail change > Notification

## Purpose
Controls the notification applied when a bank detail change is created, updated or processed. Changes apply to new records only unless the administrator selects Reapply to open records.

## Allowed values
| Value | Guidance |
|---|---|
| 30 days | Use for high-volume tenants |
| Global | Legacy behaviour |
| 15 minutes | Requires L2 enablement |
| Strict | Recommended for most tenants |

## Impact of changes
Most cases are resolved by a tenant administrator without engineering involvement. Collect the tenant ID, the affected record ID, the exact error text and the time of failure in UTC before escalating. Customers typically report this when a bank detail change is processed in bulk or immediately after an administrator changes Supplier Management settings. An incorrect value here is a common contributor to E-5379 and E-5148.

## Audit
Every change to this setting is recorded in the admin audit log with the acting administrator's role and a timestamp.
