# FAQ: Can a certificate expiry alert be restored after deletion?

## Answer
Retention follows the tenant's data retention policy, which is 7 years by default.

## Details
Most cases are resolved by a tenant administrator without engineering involvement. Collect the tenant ID, the affected record ID, the exact error text and the time of failure in UTC before escalating. This article applies to tenants on release 26.1 and later. Earlier releases display the same behaviour but use a different admin menu path.
