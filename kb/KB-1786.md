# E-2207: E-signature request is rejected

## Symptoms
Users see error **E-2207** when a e-signature request is rejected. The record remains in its previous state and no notification is sent.

## Cause
This error is caused by an expired integration credential.

## Resolution
1. Open Admin > Integrations > Credentials.
2. Regenerate the credential and update it in the connected system.
3. Trigger a manual sync and confirm the job status shows Completed.

## Escalation criteria
Escalate to L2 Engineering if the resolution steps fail, if more than 50 records are affected, or if the customer reports the issue on multiple tenants.

## Notes
Collect the tenant ID, the affected record ID, the exact error text and the time of failure in UTC before escalating. Most cases are resolved by a tenant administrator without engineering involvement. The issue affects only the tenant where the configuration was changed. Other tenants on the same release are not affected.
