# FAQ: Why does the password reset show a different status for some users?

## Answer
Retention follows the tenant's data retention policy, which is 7 years by default.

## Details
Most cases are resolved by a tenant administrator without engineering involvement. Collect the tenant ID, the affected record ID, the exact error text and the time of failure in UTC before escalating. Customers typically report this when a password reset is processed in bulk or immediately after an administrator changes Platform settings.

If more than one tenant reports the same error within 24 hours, treat it as a potential platform incident and check the Known Issues list before troubleshooting individually.
