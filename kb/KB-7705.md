# E-7336: Master data import creates duplicate records

## Symptoms
Users see error **E-7336** when a master data import creates duplicate records. The record remains in its previous state and no notification is sent.

## Cause
This error is caused by a missing role permission for the acting user.

## Resolution
1. Open Admin > Roles and locate the role of the affected user.
2. Grant the permission listed in the error detail.
3. Ask the user to refresh the session and retry.

## Escalation criteria
Escalate to L2 Engineering if the resolution steps fail, if more than 50 records are affected, or if the customer reports the issue on multiple tenants.

## Notes
Collect the tenant ID, the affected record ID, the exact error text and the time of failure in UTC before escalating. If more than one tenant reports the same error within 24 hours, treat it as a potential platform incident and check the Known Issues list before troubleshooting individually. Most cases are resolved by a tenant administrator without engineering involvement.
