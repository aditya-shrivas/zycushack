# Handling suspected data exposure between suppliers or tenants

## Scope
Any report that a user, supplier or tenant can see data they should not see: bids, prices, invoices, contracts, bank details or personal data.

## Handling
1. Classify as Security, priority P1, regardless of the customer's stated priority or tone.
2. Route to the Security team immediately and set escalate = true.
3. Do not attempt to reproduce the issue in the customer tenant.
4. Reply to the customer acknowledging receipt and stating that the Security team is engaged. Do not speculate on cause or scope.
5. For sourcing events, advise the customer to pause the affected event until Security confirms containment.
