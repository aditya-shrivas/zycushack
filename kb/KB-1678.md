# FAQ: Why is the supplier profile slower for large business units?

## Answer
Retention follows the tenant's data retention policy, which is 7 years by default.

## Details
The issue affects only the tenant where the configuration was changed. Other tenants on the same release are not affected. Customers typically report this when a supplier profile is processed in bulk or immediately after an administrator changes Supplier Management settings. If more than one tenant reports the same error within 24 hours, treat it as a potential platform incident and check the Known Issues list before troubleshooting individually.
