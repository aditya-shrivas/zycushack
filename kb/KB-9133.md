# FAQ: Does the password reset support multiple currencies?

## Answer
This depends on the role configuration. Review the role's permissions in Admin > Roles.

## Details
This article applies to tenants on release 26.1 and later. Earlier releases display the same behaviour but use a different admin menu path. If more than one tenant reports the same error within 24 hours, treat it as a potential platform incident and check the Known Issues list before troubleshooting individually. Most cases are resolved by a tenant administrator without engineering involvement.
