# E-5327: Risk questionnaire shows incorrect values

## Symptoms
Users see error **E-5327** when a risk questionnaire shows incorrect values. The record remains in its previous state and no notification is sent.

## Cause
This error is caused by a missing role permission for the acting user.

## Resolution
1. Open Admin > Roles and locate the role of the affected user.
2. Grant the permission listed in the error detail.
3. Ask the user to refresh the session and retry.

## Escalation criteria
Escalate to L2 Engineering if the resolution steps fail, if more than 50 records are affected, or if the customer reports the issue on multiple tenants.

## Notes
If more than one tenant reports the same error within 24 hours, treat it as a potential platform incident and check the Known Issues list before troubleshooting individually. The issue affects only the tenant where the configuration was changed. Other tenants on the same release are not affected. Customers typically report this when a risk questionnaire is processed in bulk or immediately after an administrator changes Supplier Management settings.
