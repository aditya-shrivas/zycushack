# FAQ: Why is the 3-way match slower for large business units?

## Answer
Retention follows the tenant's data retention policy, which is 7 years by default.

## Details
Customers typically report this when a 3-way match is processed in bulk or immediately after an administrator changes eInvoice settings. The issue affects only the tenant where the configuration was changed. Other tenants on the same release are not affected. Collect the tenant ID, the affected record ID, the exact error text and the time of failure in UTC before escalating.

If more than one tenant reports the same error within 24 hours, treat it as a potential platform incident and check the Known Issues list before troubleshooting individually. This article applies to tenants on release 26.1 and later. Earlier releases display the same behaviour but use a different admin menu path.
