# E-5176: Risk questionnaire creates duplicate records

## Symptoms
Users see error **E-5176** when a risk questionnaire creates duplicate records. The record remains in its previous state and no notification is sent.

## Cause
This error is caused by a defect introduced in release 26.2.

## Resolution
1. Apply the workaround documented in the release notes for 26.2.
2. Raise an L2 ticket referencing this article if the workaround fails.
3. Monitor for the hotfix announcement.

## Escalation criteria
Escalate to L2 Engineering if the resolution steps fail, if more than 50 records are affected, or if the customer reports the issue on multiple tenants.

## Notes
Most cases are resolved by a tenant administrator without engineering involvement. Collect the tenant ID, the affected record ID, the exact error text and the time of failure in UTC before escalating. This article applies to tenants on release 26.1 and later. Earlier releases display the same behaviour but use a different admin menu path.
