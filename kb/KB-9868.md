# E-7342: Po export times out

## Symptoms
Users see error **E-7342** when a PO export times out. The record remains in its previous state and no notification is sent.

## Cause
This error is caused by an exchange-rate table that was not refreshed.

## Resolution
1. Open Admin > Currency and check the last refresh date.
2. Run Refresh Rates now.
3. Re-run the affected calculation or report.

## Escalation criteria
Escalate to L2 Engineering if the resolution steps fail, if more than 50 records are affected, or if the customer reports the issue on multiple tenants.

## Notes
Most cases are resolved by a tenant administrator without engineering involvement. Customers typically report this when a PO export is processed in bulk or immediately after an administrator changes Integrations settings. Collect the tenant ID, the affected record ID, the exact error text and the time of failure in UTC before escalating.
