# FAQ: Why does the classification job show a different status for some users?

## Answer
Yes. An administrator can enable this under the module settings; the change applies to new records.

## Details
The issue affects only the tenant where the configuration was changed. Other tenants on the same release are not affected. Most cases are resolved by a tenant administrator without engineering involvement. Customers typically report this when a classification job is processed in bulk or immediately after an administrator changes Spend Analytics settings.

Collect the tenant ID, the affected record ID, the exact error text and the time of failure in UTC before escalating. If more than one tenant reports the same error within 24 hours, treat it as a potential platform incident and check the Known Issues list before troubleshooting individually.
