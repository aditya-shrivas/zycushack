# FAQ: Who receives notifications for a payment file?

## Answer
Yes. An administrator can enable this under the module settings; the change applies to new records.

## Details
Most cases are resolved by a tenant administrator without engineering involvement. Customers typically report this when a payment file is processed in bulk or immediately after an administrator changes eInvoice settings. Collect the tenant ID, the affected record ID, the exact error text and the time of failure in UTC before escalating.

This article applies to tenants on release 26.1 and later. Earlier releases display the same behaviour but use a different admin menu path. Most cases are resolved by a tenant administrator without engineering involvement. Collect the tenant ID, the affected record ID, the exact error text and the time of failure in UTC before escalating.
