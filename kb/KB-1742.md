# FAQ: Who receives notifications for a supplier invitation?

## Answer
Yes. An administrator can enable this under the module settings; the change applies to new records.

## Details
If more than one tenant reports the same error within 24 hours, treat it as a potential platform incident and check the Known Issues list before troubleshooting individually. Collect the tenant ID, the affected record ID, the exact error text and the time of failure in UTC before escalating. Most cases are resolved by a tenant administrator without engineering involvement.
