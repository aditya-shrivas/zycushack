# KNOWN ISSUE: Invoice OCR jobs fail with E-4102 after release 26.2.3

## Status
**Active incident.** Engineering is working on a hotfix. Incident reference: INC-26-0418.

## Impact
Invoice OCR jobs on tenants upgraded to 26.2.3 move to Failed with error E-4102. Multiple tenants are affected.

## Workaround
1. Switch affected invoice channels to Manual Entry mode (Admin > eInvoice > Channels).
2. Hold month-end OCR batches until the hotfix is deployed, or process critical invoices manually.

## Support handling
- Treat new E-4102 reports as P1 if all OCR processing is blocked for the tenant.
- Link the ticket to INC-26-0418 and route to L2 Engineering.
- Do not ask customers to clear caches or re-upload; it does not help.
- Notify the TAM for Premium accounts.
