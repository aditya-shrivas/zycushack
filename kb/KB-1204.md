# How to: Change a PO after approval

## Before you start
Customers typically report this when a record is processed in bulk or immediately after an administrator changes eProcurement settings. Most cases are resolved by a tenant administrator without engineering involvement. Collect the tenant ID, the affected record ID, the exact error text and the time of failure in UTC before escalating.

## Steps
1. Sign in as a eProcurement administrator.
2. Go to eProcurement > Settings.
3. Select the option for: change a po after approval.
4. Enter the required values and save.
5. Verify the change on a test record before announcing it to users.
