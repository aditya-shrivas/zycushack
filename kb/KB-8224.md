# FAQ: Can we restrict who edits a password reset?

## Answer
Retention follows the tenant's data retention policy, which is 7 years by default.

## Details
Most cases are resolved by a tenant administrator without engineering involvement. Customers typically report this when a password reset is processed in bulk or immediately after an administrator changes Platform settings. This article applies to tenants on release 26.1 and later. Earlier releases display the same behaviour but use a different admin menu path.
