# E-4385: 3-way match times out

## Symptoms
Users see error **E-4385** when a 3-way match times out. The record remains in its previous state and no notification is sent.

## Cause
This error is caused by a missing role permission for the acting user.

## Resolution
1. Open Admin > Roles and locate the role of the affected user.
2. Grant the permission listed in the error detail.
3. Ask the user to refresh the session and retry.

## Escalation criteria
Escalate to L2 Engineering if the resolution steps fail, if more than 50 records are affected, or if the customer reports the issue on multiple tenants.

## Notes
The issue affects only the tenant where the configuration was changed. Other tenants on the same release are not affected. Most cases are resolved by a tenant administrator without engineering involvement. Collect the tenant ID, the affected record ID, the exact error text and the time of failure in UTC before escalating.
