# E-8256: Sso login does not trigger

## Symptoms
Users see error **E-8256** when a SSO login does not trigger. The record remains in its previous state and no notification is sent.

## Cause
This error is caused by a workflow rule referencing an inactive approver group.

## Resolution
1. Open the workflow rule named in the error detail.
2. Replace the inactive approver group with an active group.
3. Re-route stuck documents from Workflow > Pending.

## Escalation criteria
Escalate to L2 Engineering if the resolution steps fail, if more than 50 records are affected, or if the customer reports the issue on multiple tenants.

## Notes
Customers typically report this when a SSO login is processed in bulk or immediately after an administrator changes Platform settings. Collect the tenant ID, the affected record ID, the exact error text and the time of failure in UTC before escalating. Most cases are resolved by a tenant administrator without engineering involvement.
