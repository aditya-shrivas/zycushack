# How to: Configure session timeout

## Before you start
If more than one tenant reports the same error within 24 hours, treat it as a potential platform incident and check the Known Issues list before troubleshooting individually. Most cases are resolved by a tenant administrator without engineering involvement. Collect the tenant ID, the affected record ID, the exact error text and the time of failure in UTC before escalating.

## Steps
1. Sign in as a Platform administrator.
2. Go to Platform > Settings.
3. Select the option for: configure session timeout.
4. Enter the required values and save.
5. Verify the change on a test record before announcing it to users.
