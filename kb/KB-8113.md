# E-5379: Bank detail change stays stuck in processing

## Symptoms
Users see error **E-5379** when a bank detail change stays stuck in processing. The record remains in its previous state and no notification is sent.

## Cause
This error is caused by a field value exceeding the allowed length.

## Resolution
1. Identify the field named in the error detail.
2. Shorten the value to the documented limit or request a limit increase.
3. Resubmit the record.

## Escalation criteria
Escalate to L2 Engineering if the resolution steps fail, if more than 50 records are affected, or if the customer reports the issue on multiple tenants.

## Notes
Customers typically report this when a bank detail change is processed in bulk or immediately after an administrator changes Supplier Management settings. Collect the tenant ID, the affected record ID, the exact error text and the time of failure in UTC before escalating. The issue affects only the tenant where the configuration was changed. Other tenants on the same release are not affected.
