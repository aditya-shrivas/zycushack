# FAQ: Can we restrict who edits a PO export?

## Answer
This depends on the role configuration. Review the role's permissions in Admin > Roles.

## Details
Collect the tenant ID, the affected record ID, the exact error text and the time of failure in UTC before escalating. If more than one tenant reports the same error within 24 hours, treat it as a potential platform incident and check the Known Issues list before troubleshooting individually. Customers typically report this when a PO export is processed in bulk or immediately after an administrator changes Integrations settings.

The issue affects only the tenant where the configuration was changed. Other tenants on the same release are not affected. Most cases are resolved by a tenant administrator without engineering involvement. Collect the tenant ID, the affected record ID, the exact error text and the time of failure in UTC before escalating.
