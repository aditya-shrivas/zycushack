# FAQ: Can we export bid submission data on a schedule?

## Answer
Yes. An administrator can enable this under the module settings; the change applies to new records.

## Details
Customers typically report this when a bid submission is processed in bulk or immediately after an administrator changes Sourcing settings. Collect the tenant ID, the affected record ID, the exact error text and the time of failure in UTC before escalating. If more than one tenant reports the same error within 24 hours, treat it as a potential platform incident and check the Known Issues list before troubleshooting individually.

Most cases are resolved by a tenant administrator without engineering involvement.
