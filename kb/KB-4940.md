# FAQ: Can a bid submission be restored after deletion?

## Answer
Yes. An administrator can enable this under the module settings; the change applies to new records.

## Details
The issue affects only the tenant where the configuration was changed. Other tenants on the same release are not affected. Most cases are resolved by a tenant administrator without engineering involvement. Collect the tenant ID, the affected record ID, the exact error text and the time of failure in UTC before escalating.
