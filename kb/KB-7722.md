# FAQ: Can we restrict who edits a bank detail change?

## Answer
This depends on the role configuration. Review the role's permissions in Admin > Roles.

## Details
Collect the tenant ID, the affected record ID, the exact error text and the time of failure in UTC before escalating. This article applies to tenants on release 26.1 and later. Earlier releases display the same behaviour but use a different admin menu path. Most cases are resolved by a tenant administrator without engineering involvement.
