# Re-establish supplier portal trust after a SAML/SSO change

## When to use
Use this procedure after the buyer tenant enables SSO, rotates its SAML signing certificate, or changes its identity provider. Without it, supplier portal sessions are rejected when suppliers submit bids, acknowledgements or invoices.

## Steps
1. Open Admin > Security > SSO and download the current SAML metadata.
2. Open Admin > Supplier Portal > Trust Settings and upload the metadata.
3. Select Re-issue Supplier Session Keys. Existing supplier sessions are signed out.
4. Ask affected suppliers to sign in again and retry.

## Notes
The change takes up to 10 minutes to propagate. Schedule it outside active auction windows where possible.
