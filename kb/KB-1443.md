# How to: Review classification results

## Before you start
Most cases are resolved by a tenant administrator without engineering involvement. Customers typically report this when a record is processed in bulk or immediately after an administrator changes Spend Analytics settings. Collect the tenant ID, the affected record ID, the exact error text and the time of failure in UTC before escalating.

## Steps
1. Sign in as a Spend Analytics administrator.
2. Go to Spend Analytics > Settings.
3. Select the option for: review classification results.
4. Enter the required values and save.
5. Verify the change on a test record before announcing it to users.
