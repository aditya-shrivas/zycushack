# E-8225: Role permission creates duplicate records

## Symptoms
Users see error **E-8225** when a role permission creates duplicate records. The record remains in its previous state and no notification is sent.

## Cause
This error is caused by a field mapping that does not match the tenant configuration.

## Resolution
1. Open Admin > Field Mapping and compare the source field list with the tenant schema.
2. Correct the mismatched field and save the mapping.
3. Re-run the failed transaction from the error queue.

## Escalation criteria
Escalate to L2 Engineering if the resolution steps fail, if more than 50 records are affected, or if the customer reports the issue on multiple tenants.

## Notes
Collect the tenant ID, the affected record ID, the exact error text and the time of failure in UTC before escalating. If more than one tenant reports the same error within 24 hours, treat it as a potential platform incident and check the Known Issues list before troubleshooting individually. Customers typically report this when a role permission is processed in bulk or immediately after an administrator changes Platform settings.
