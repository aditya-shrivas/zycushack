# FAQ: Can we export invoice approval data on a schedule?

## Answer
Yes. An administrator can enable this under the module settings; the change applies to new records.

## Details
Customers typically report this when a invoice approval is processed in bulk or immediately after an administrator changes eInvoice settings. Collect the tenant ID, the affected record ID, the exact error text and the time of failure in UTC before escalating. The issue affects only the tenant where the configuration was changed. Other tenants on the same release are not affected.
