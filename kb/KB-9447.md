# FAQ: Can we restrict who edits a bid submission?

## Answer
This depends on the role configuration. Review the role's permissions in Admin > Roles.

## Details
Collect the tenant ID, the affected record ID, the exact error text and the time of failure in UTC before escalating. If more than one tenant reports the same error within 24 hours, treat it as a potential platform incident and check the Known Issues list before troubleshooting individually. Customers typically report this when a bid submission is processed in bulk or immediately after an administrator changes Sourcing settings.
